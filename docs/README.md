## 🏛️ Executive Summary

Academic diploma fraud and unauthorized transcript alteration cause massive financial and reputational damage worldwide. **Certificate Verifier** replaces easily forgeable paper diplomas and vulnerable centralized databases with immutable Ethereum blockchain ledger records:

1. **Tamper-Proof On-Chain Storage**: Academic degrees, student identities, and certificate serial numbers are cryptographically anchored into a Solidity smart contract.
2. **Instant Multi-Modal Verification**: Verifiers can inspect authenticity via SHA-256 hash lookup, dynamic QR code camera scan, or document upload.
3. **Decentralized Consensus**: Verification requires zero trust in centralized servers; proofs are independently validated against the Ethereum blockchain state.
4. **Student Ownership**: Students can view, download high-resolution verified PDF certificates, and share verifiable credentials globally.
